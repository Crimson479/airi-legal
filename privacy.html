Airi Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

HomeTerms

Airi is an AI-powered Discord application designed to provide chat assistance, web research, image and document understanding, translation, reminders, memory features, and server utilities.

1. Information Airi May Process

  • Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, and related technical identifiers.
  • Messages, commands, and prompts sent directly to Airi.
  • Images, screenshots, and documents intentionally submitted for analysis.
  • User preferences, reminders, profile facts, and conversation memories created through Airi's features.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide Airi's features, maintain user-specific conversation context, deliver reminders, improve response relevance, and perform requested server functions.

3. AI and Search Services

Content submitted to AI or research features may be processed by third-party services used by Airi, including Google Gemini and Tavily, when necessary to answer a request.

4. Memory Privacy

Saved conversation memories are separated by Discord server and user. Airi is designed not to provide one user's private stored memories to another user.

5. Sensitive Information

Users should not send passwords, authentication tokens, one-time passwords, payment card details, private API keys, or other highly sensitive credentials to Airi.

6. Data Retention

Stored preferences, reminders, profile facts, and memory information may remain in Airi's database until deleted by the user, removed by the bot owner, or deleted as part of service maintenance.

7. User Controls

Users may use Airi's available memory, profile, reminder, and deletion commands to review or remove supported stored information.

8. Security

Reasonable efforts are made to protect stored information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Airi's features or data practices change.
